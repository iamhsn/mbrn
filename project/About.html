Our Story

Rooted in heritage, packed with care.

Mebran began with a simple belief — that the dates and dry fruits enjoyed for centuries deserve to be sourced honestly, handled gently and delivered fresh.

FOUNDERS · LIFESTYLEat the souk · selecting dates by hand
How It Started

A search for the real thing.

It started on a trip to Madinah, holding an Ajwa date so soft and rich it bore no resemblance to what lined the shelves back home.

We kept asking the same question: why was it so hard to find dates and dry fruits this good in India? So we went to the source — building relationships with growers across the Middle East and beyond, learning how the best fruit is grown, graded and stored. Mebran is the result: a small, obsessive company that refuses to compromise on freshness or quality.

The Journey

Milestones along the way.

2019

The first box

Mebran launches with a single offering — premium Ajwa dates from Madinah, packed by hand and shipped to a handful of customers who became believers.

2021

Beyond dates

We expand into gourmet dry fruits and nuts — Turkish figs, Iranian pistachios, Mamra almonds — applying the same single-origin standard to everything we sell.

2023

The gifting era

Our rigid gift boxes and corporate hampers are born, quickly becoming a Ramadan and festive favourite for families and companies across India.

2026

Fresh to every doorstep

With nationwide dispatch and small-batch packing, Mebran now delivers premium goodness to every corner of the country — freshness guaranteed.

From The Source

We go to the grove.

Great fruit can't be sourced from a screen. We visit the regions we buy from, build long-term relationships and select by hand.

From the date palms of Madinah to the fig orchards of Turkey and the pistachio farms of Iran, our standard is the same everywhere: single-origin, Grade A, and sealed at the peak of freshness.
