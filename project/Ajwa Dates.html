Ajwa is the most revered of all date varieties — small, dark and soft with a fine, almost velvety skin. Each fruit carries a deep, mellow sweetness balanced by subtle notes of caramel and dried fruit.

Our Ajwa is grown in the palm groves of Madinah, hand-harvested at peak ripeness, then graded and sealed within hours to lock in moisture and flavour. The result is a date that stays soft, plump and luxuriously rich from the first piece to the last.

  • Naturally rich in fibre to support everyday digestion
  • A natural source of potassium & magnesium
  • Slow-release energy from naturally occurring sugars
  • No added sugar, preservatives or artificial colours

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Once opened, keep refrigerated in an airtight container to preserve softness.

Best enjoyed within 6 months of packing. A natural sugar bloom may appear on the skin over time — this is harmless and a sign of pure, unadulterated fruit.
