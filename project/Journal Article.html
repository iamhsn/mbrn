The Ajwa smoothie bowl that starts our mornings

By the Mebran KitchenJune 20265 min read
COVER · LIFESTYLEajwa smoothie bowl · overhead · linen

There's a reason this bowl has become our most-requested breakfast at the Mebran studio: it's naturally sweet, deeply satisfying and comes together in the time it takes the kettle to boil.

Premium Ajwa dates do the heavy lifting here. Their soft, caramel-rich flesh blends into a velvety base that needs no added sugar — just fruit, a little nut butter and whatever you have in the fridge. It's the kind of breakfast that feels indulgent but quietly keeps you going until lunch.

Why dates make the best base

Unlike refined sweeteners, dates bring fibre and minerals along with their sweetness, which means a steadier release of energy and none of the mid-morning slump. They also lend a natural creaminess that you simply can't get from sugar or syrup.

Naturally rich, soft and deeply satisfying — the way premium dates have always been enjoyed.
STEP · MACROblending the date base

The recipe

Serves two. Scale the toppings to your liking — this is more of a template than a rulebook.

You'll need

  • 6 premium Ajwa dates, pitted
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 150ml milk of choice
  • Handful of ice
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Method

  • Soak the dates in warm water for five minutes if they're firm, then drain.
  • Blend the dates, banana, almond butter, milk, ice and cinnamon until thick and smooth.
  • Pour into bowls and smooth the top with the back of a spoon.
  • Finish with chopped Mamra almonds, pistachios, a few sliced dates and a drizzle of nut butter.

Make it yours

Swap the banana for mango in summer, add a spoon of cocoa for a chocolate version, or stir through a little yoghurt for extra protein. However you build it, the Ajwa base keeps it grounded and naturally sweet.
